Privacy Policy

Tiny Tile Merge by Tiny Tile Studio
Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Tiny Tile Merge ("the game", "the app") handles information. Tiny Tile Studio ("we", "us") made the game.

Summary

The game does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. It works fully offline and does not require an account.

Information the game stores on your device

To work properly, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • Your best score
  • Your settings (difficulty level, theme, and sound on or off)
  • A gameplay log (moves, scores and timing of recent games), which is used only if you choose to copy it yourself using the "Copy play log" button

This information never leaves your device unless you copy and share it yourself. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or any other data from the game. The game does not request any special permissions on your device.

Third parties

The current version of the game does not include advertising, analytics or any third-party services that collect data. If we add advertising or in-app purchases in the future, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety information before releasing that version, and will describe what is collected and by whom.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. Please check this page for updates.
